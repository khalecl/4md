# Energy Audit Programme 2026
Building performance review for the operations team

## Why Audit
- **Cost**: energy is 30% of operating spend
- **Compliance**: SASO and ISO 50001 requirements
- **Carbon**: Scope 2 reductions for ESG reporting
- **Reliability**: find failing equipment early

## Audit Process
1. Scope the site
2. Collect meter data
3. Survey equipment
4. Analyse savings
5. Report and verify

## Roadmap
- Q1: Baseline and metering
- Q2: Quick wins in lighting and HVAC controls
- Q3: Chiller optimisation
- Q4: Measurement and verification

## Key Messages
- Most savings come from controls, not new equipment
- Metering is the foundation of every decision
- Staff behaviour drives a fifth of consumption
- Payback under two years is common

## Workstreams
- HVAC
    - Chiller sequencing
    - AHU schedules
- Lighting
    - LED retrofit
    - Occupancy sensors
- Metering
    - Sub-meters per floor
    - BMS trending

## Implementation Steps
1. Hold a kick-off meeting with facilities, finance and operations to agree the audit boundaries and data access
2. Install temporary loggers on the main incomers and the chiller plant for at least two weeks
3. Walk through every plant room and record nameplate data with photographs
4. Build the baseline model and test it against twelve months of utility bills

## A Quote
> Energy efficiency is the cheapest source of new capacity.
